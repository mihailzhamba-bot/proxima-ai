Wake goes to Paperclip; Bridge is not a scheduler. Runner is invoked for a queued
approved job via runner.py --config <trusted file> --job <job ID> on Harper.
Install loop-runner.service on Harper for --serve consumption of queued commands.
Bridge admits one heavy execution at a time; unknown work requires reconciliation. Director
sees the Bridge run_id and generation in its trusted wake input. Unknown dispatch is
never repeated, even after the upstream 24h idempotency expiry. Check remote run lists
and adopt a confirmed external ID with operator credentials. A dispatching job cannot
be claimed a second time; inspect deterministic OpenHands conversation and its saved
bundle. An uncertain PR is recovered by its fixed branch plus verified SHA only.

Cancellation fences locally before network. Paperclip cancel is Board-only and does
not imply Hermes stop. Bridge explicitly stops Hermes and pauses OpenHands, then
reads their actual states. No confirmation means CANCELLING, not stopped. A delayed
Hermes child of a cancelled parent cannot obtain a lease. SSE is a status snapshot,
not replay; Paperclip event cursor is durable. Heavy verification never runs here.
